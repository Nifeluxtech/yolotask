Refund & Dispute Policy

Last updated: October 2026
Core principle: Advertisers only pay for verified work. Earners only get paid for verified work. YOLOTASK holds funds in escrow to guarantee both.

1. Advertiser Refunds

  • Pre-LIVE cancellation: 100% of budget returns to your wallet.
  • Unfilled campaigns: remaining locked budget auto-refunds at expiry.
  • Rejected tasks: funds for correctly-rejected submissions return to your wallet.

Refunds are credited to your YOLOTASK wallet (not reversed to card) to protect against chargeback abuse.

2. Earner Disputes

  • Unfair rejection? Open a dispute via Support with your proof link.
  • Our Reviewer team inspects manually; correct work is paid from platform reserves.

3. Withdrawal Disputes

  • Failed transfers due to wrong bank details return to your wallet within 48 hours.
  • YOLOTASK is not liable for Nigerian banking network or Paystack processing delays.

4. Contact Support

support@nifelux.com — include your User ID and screenshots for fastest resolution.
