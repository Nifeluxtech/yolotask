Refund & Dispute Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Core Principle: Advertisers only pay for verified work. Earners only get paid for verified work. YOLOTASK holds funds in secure escrow to guarantee this.

1. Advertiser Refunds

  • Campaign Cancellation: If an Advertiser cancels a campaign before it goes LIVE, 100% of the funds are returned to their YOLOTASK wallet.
  • Unfilled Campaigns: If a campaign expires and the target number of workers is not reached, the remaining locked budget is automatically refunded to the Advertiser's wallet.
  • Rejected Tasks: If an Advertiser rejects a worker's submission with valid proof of non-completion, the funds for that specific task are returned to the Advertiser's wallet.

Note: For security and to prevent Paystack chargeback fees, refunds are credited to the user's YOLOTASK wallet, not reversed to the original bank card.

2. Earner Disputes

  • If an Earner believes their task was unfairly rejected, they can open a dispute via the Support section.
  • Our dedicated Reviewer team will manually inspect the proof link. If the work was done correctly, the Earner will be paid from the platform's reserve fund.

3. Withdrawal Disputes

  • If a withdrawal fails due to incorrect bank details provided by the user, the funds will be returned to their YOLOTASK wallet after 48 hours.
  • YOLOTASK is not liable for delays caused by Nigerian banking network failures or Paystack processing times.

4. Contact Support

To open a dispute or request a refund, please email support@yolotask.com with your User ID and relevant screenshots.
