Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following information:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and gender.
  • Financial Data: Bank account details (Account Name, Number, Bank) required for processing withdrawals. Payments are processed securely via Paystack.
  • Task Data: Social media handles and proof links submitted to verify task completion.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process transactions, withdrawals, and campaign funding.
  • To match Earners with targeted campaigns based on interests and demographics.
  • To send platform announcements, security alerts, and support messages.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary information with:

  • Advertisers: Only the proof links and social handles required to verify that a task was completed.
  • Paystack: To process secure financial transactions.
  • Law Enforcement: If required by Nigerian law or to prevent fraud.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and secure database practices (Supabase) to protect your data. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your data or request account deletion by contacting our support team. Note that financial transaction records are kept for 5 years as required by Nigerian financial regulations.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@yolotask.com.
