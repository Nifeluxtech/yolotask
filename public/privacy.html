Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone, gender.
  • Financial data: bank details required for payouts (processed securely by Paystack).
  • Task data: social handles and proof links submitted for verification.

2. How We Use It

  • Processing transactions, withdrawals and campaign funding.
  • Matching earners to targeted campaigns via interests and demographics.
  • Sending announcements, security alerts and support messages.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share only what is necessary with:

  • Advertisers: proof links and handles needed to verify tasks.
  • Paystack: secure payment processing.
  • Law enforcement: when required by Nigerian law or to prevent fraud.

4. Data Security

Industry-standard TLS encryption, hashed passwords, and role-restricted database access (Supabase + Vercel).

5. Your Rights

Request a copy of your data or account deletion via support. Financial records are retained for 5 years as required by Nigerian regulation.
