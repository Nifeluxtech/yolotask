FAQs

Good questions deserve clear answers.

YOLOTASK is a task marketplace. The money flows are payments for activation, campaign funding, approved task rewards, referrals, and withdrawals.

Create an account ↗
What does an earner pay?

Earners pay a one-time ₦1,000 activation fee. Earning features unlock only after Paystack verification.

Can advertisers register for free?

Yes. Advertisers register free and fund their wallet before creating a campaign.

How does a task get approved?

The earner submits the requested proof. The campaign owner or admin reviews it and the server posts an approved reward to the ledger.

How are audiences targeted?

Advertisers can choose a general audience or target interests and gender without seeing private user records.

Is YOLOTASK an investment platform?

No. YOLOTASK is a task-to-earn and digital-promotion marketplace. Earners are paid for approved work.
