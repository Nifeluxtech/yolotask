How YOLOTASK works

A straightforward task marketplace journey.

YOLOTASK is designed so the action, the review, and the reward are visible—not hidden behind complicated product language.

The journey

Four steps from sign-up to outcome.

The platform keeps the experience simple for both sides of the marketplace.

1

Create your profile

Choose earner or advertiser. Earners add a gender and at least three interests for relevance.

2

Choose your lane

Earners activate once through verified Paystack payment. Advertisers register free and fund their wallet.

3

Take action

Complete a matched task with the required proof, or publish a moderated campaign.

4

Track progress

Follow submissions, wallet ledger entries, campaign status, referrals, reputation, and support.
