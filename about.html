About YOLOTASK

A marketplace built around useful digital action.

YOLOTASK is a task-to-earn and digital-promotion marketplace. It is not an investment platform: earners are rewarded for approved tasks, while advertisers pay to run campaigns.

What we do

Make digital promotion more accountable.

YOLOTASK brings earners and advertisers into one clear marketplace. Earners complete approved promotional tasks. Advertisers fund campaigns to reach relevant audiences.

✓

For earners

Discover tasks that match your interests, submit proof, track review, and receive approved rewards.

↗

For advertisers

Build a campaign, choose an audience, reserve your budget, and monitor the result.

◈

For admins

Moderate activity, manage pricing and task types, review withdrawals, and protect the platform.
